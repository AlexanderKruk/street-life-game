## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: single 16:9 landscape narrative event image for the existing Street Life urban survival game. Input image 1 is the authoritative protagonist identity reference. Input image 2 is the established Warsaw street scene to transform into a closing-time departure. Show the SAME ordinary late-thirties man, tousled wavy dark brown hair, short stubble, lean build, charcoal hooded field jacket over a dark sweater, dark jeans and dark boots. He has JUST LEFT a public place because it has closed and is now standing outside on the pavement. Three-quarter view of his recognizable face: tired, subdued disappointment, gathering himself and looking toward the street. One hand holds a backpack shoulder strap. Exactly ONE practical black backpack, worn on his shoulders, no extra bag on the pavement. Immediately behind him, a clearly SHUT neutral glass-and-metal public-building entrance with closed double doors and simple handles; blinds behind the glass partly lowered, indoor lights dimmed. The closed door is important but his face remains the visual focus. It should plausibly fit a shop, daytime help center or clinic, without any specific shop branding, medical symbol, home doorway, religious symbol or readable sign. No staff, police, confrontation or other foreground characters. Autumn Polish city, a few dry leaves and slightly damp textured stone paving, muted cool blue-grey late-day light, restrained warm amber practical lights further down the street. Cinematic realistic painterly game illustration, natural anatomy and detailed skin/fabric, same rendering quality as the references, dignified grounded storytelling. Medium shot including the protagonist, closed entrance and pavement, central safe framing with clear mobile readability. No bench, no sitting, no illness tissue, no text, captions, logos, UI, watermarks, labels or panels. One coherent opaque landscape image, no collage. Do not reproduce the character reference sheet.
