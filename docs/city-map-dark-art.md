# Dark city plan (v255)

Created with the built-in image generation tool from the user-approved dark mobile map mockup. The final asset contains only fictional city cartography, without interface, labels, markers or neighborhood names. It depicts a broad city from directly above, with many districts, an irregular river through the right third, bridges, parks and a railway corridor. It does not encode real destination coordinates.

Retained project source: `public/assets/city-map-dark.webp` (1024×1536, WebP quality 90). Responsive variants use widths 480, 768 and 1024 at quality 80 and method 6; they are recorded in the image manifest and optimizer. The generated PNG remains with the image service. The earlier city-night asset is retained, but is no longer used or warmed by the map.

## Generation specification

Clean uninterrupted portrait city-map background, based on the approved dark digital map direction. North-up overhead cartography, charcoal/slate street blocks, dense irregular roads with slightly brighter main roads, desaturated blue river winding through the right third, several bridges, dark green parks and long railway corridor on the left half. Show a broad fictional Polish city with many neighborhoods; flat, readable cartography at phone scale. Extend map detail into every area occupied by UI in the reference. No perspective, sky, status bar, clock, battery, title, buttons, markers, dots, labels, words, letters, numerals, neighborhood names, cards, icons, borders, frame, vignette or watermark. Keep map geography separate from actual interactive labels and routes.
