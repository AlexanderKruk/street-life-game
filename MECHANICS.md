# Street Life — Core Mechanics

## D20 checks

Use D20 only when the player knowingly chooses an uncertain or risky action. Random event spawning stays hidden and does not use the visible die.

Result: `D20 + modifier >= DC`. Natural 20 is a critical success and natural 1 is a critical failure unless a check is physically impossible.

### Requirements

Some actions have minimum requirements before a roll is allowed. Example: catching a falling phone requires at least 20 Energy and 20 Health. If the requirement is not met, the choice stays visible but disabled. A natural 20 cannot bypass an impossible physical action.

### Modifiers come from the character's real condition

Do not add abstract RPG attributes unless the design later needs them.

- **Reflex**: primarily Energy; physical checks can also require minimum Health.
- **Persuasion**: Hygiene is the main modifier and Mood is secondary. Poor Hygiene can make ordinary social negotiation harder.
- Context matters. Poor Hygiene should not automatically penalize every conversation; social workers, shelters and similar services should use context-appropriate rules.
- Future checks can combine Health, Energy, Hygiene, Mood, Documents, equipment and temporary effects.

Current Persuasion bands:
- Hygiene 80+: +2; 60–79: +1; 40–59: 0; 20–39: -2; below 20: -3.
- Mood 75+: +1; 25–74: 0; below 25: -1.

### Current examples

**Catch a falling phone — Reflex DC 11**
Requires Energy 20 and Health 20. Success prevents damage; failure damages the phone; natural 1 causes heavier damage.

**Ask for more details — Persuasion DC 12**
The `Useful tip` street event uses Hygiene + Mood. The player can avoid the roll by simply listening or walking away.

## Design rule

DC represents how difficult the situation is. The modifier represents the player's current condition and context. Prefer meaningful alternatives to mandatory dice rolls: safe choice, risky check, or walking away.
